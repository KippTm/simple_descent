# Game Context: Project Descent

## 1. Executive Summary
The game bridges the frantic, mob-clearing satisfaction of *Vampire Survivors* with the perpetual, high-stakes progression loop of *Risk of Rain 2*. 

## 2. Core Philosophy & Hooks
* **Organic Growth over Linear Unlocks:** Instead of selecting upgrades from a randomized menu upon leveling up, abilities passively grow stronger the more they're used, while Monuments let the player actively choose *how* an ability specializes. This rewards mechanical mastery and intentional playstyles.
* **Risk vs. Reward Exploration:** The map is not just an empty arena. It contains interactive "Monuments" of varying risk — some just cost time, some demand a sacrifice (health, movement speed, etc.), and some require a genuine skill check for a bigger payoff — forcing constant tactical decisions.
* **The "One More Run" Loop:** True roguelike meta-progression. Stats, levels, and raw power do not carry over between runs. The focus is entirely on the purity of the current run, encouraging experimentation without the grind.

## 3. Aesthetic & Vibe
* **Visual Style:** Minimalist, simple pixel art. The focus is on readability, given the sheer volume of enemies that will eventually populate the screen. Clear silhouettes for the player, distinct color coding for enemy tiers, and highly legible projectile effects are paramount.
* **Camera & Controls:** Top-down 2D perspective. Designed primarily for simple controls where the player has to focus mainly on positioning and dodging.
